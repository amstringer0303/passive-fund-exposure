What do the exposure tiers mean?
⚡ Direct — Nasdaq-100

Directly tracks the Nasdaq-100. Subject to fast-entry rule effective May 1, 2026.

⚠ Likely exposure

Large-cap growth or tech-sector funds. Likely to pick up large-cap mega-cap IPOs at next reconstitution.

⏱ Different timing

S&P 500 and total-market funds. Exposure arrives later, under different rules (12-month seasoning, profitability for S&P 500).

↩ Indirect

Target-date and fund-of-funds. Exposure passes through underlying index funds — check which ones your plan actually holds.

○ Low / zero

International, bond, and dividend funds. U.S. equity index rule changes have minimal or no direct effect.
