What changed

Two indexes. Two very different timelines.

Nasdaq-100 · Effective May 1, 2026
Fast-entry rule adopted

Top-40 mega-cap IPOs assessed on day ~7 after listing. Added on day ~15. Low-float names enter at a capped weight and ramp at subsequent reconstitutions.

Affects QQQ · QQQM
S&P 500 · No change
12-month seasoning still required

S&P Dow Jones has not adopted fast-entry. Requires 12 months of trading history plus GAAP profitability. SpaceX's S&P 500 debut would be years away, not weeks.

Affects VOO · IVV · SPY differently
SpaceX · Reported listing ~June 12
First fast-entry test case

Reported Nasdaq listing target: June 12 (ticker SPCX). Based on press reporting — not yet SEC-confirmed. If it lists and qualifies, QQQ holders could own it by early July.
