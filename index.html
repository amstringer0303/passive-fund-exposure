What changed On May 1, 2026, the Nasdaq-100 adopted a fast-entry rule: a qualifying mega-cap IPO can be added to the index — and to funds like QQQ — within roughly 15 trading days of listing. SpaceX (reported ticker: SPCX) has been reported to target a June 12 Nasdaq listing. Full explainer →

How the rule affects different funds differently

Fast-entry applies
QQQ / QQQM Directly tracks Nasdaq-100. A qualifying IPO appears here first.
VUG · SCHG · IWF Large-cap growth. Likely to pick up large-cap IPOs at next reconstitution.
VGT · XLK · FTEC Tech sector. New IT-classified listings enter at quarterly MSCI / S&P DJI review.
Different timeline
VOO · IVV · SPY S&P 500 requires 12 months of trading + GAAP profitability. No fast-entry.
VTI · ITOT · SCHB Total market. Follows CRSP / S&P DJI quarterly schedule, not Nasdaq's.
2045 Fund · Freedom 2050 Target-date funds hold other funds. Exposure depends on what's inside.
