What changed On May 1, 2026, the Nasdaq-100 adopted a fast-entry rule: a qualifying mega-cap IPO can be added to the index — and to funds like QQQ — within roughly 15 trading days of listing. SpaceX (reported ticker: SPCX) has been reported to target a June 12 Nasdaq listing. Full explainer →
