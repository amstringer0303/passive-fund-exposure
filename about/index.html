About

What this site is, who built it, and what it is not.

What this site is

Passive Fund Exposure is an educational resource for retirement savers who want to understand how index-rule changes affect what their passive funds hold. It was built in response to the Nasdaq-100's adoption of a "fast-entry" rule for large-cap, low-float IPOs in May 2026 — a change that can cause new companies to appear in funds like QQQ and QQQM within weeks of their listing.

The site provides:

  • A plain-language explainer of index methodology and the fast-entry rule
  • A client-side Exposure Analyzer that matches your fund list against a curated universe of fund-to-index mappings
  • A sourced timeline of methodology changes, IPO filings, and index events
  • A free RSS feed and email list for updates

What this site is not

This site does not provide investment advice, financial advice, legal advice, or tax advice. It does not recommend any action with respect to any fund, security, or asset. It does not characterize any fund, index, or investment strategy as appropriate or inappropriate for any particular investor. It does not assess anyone's fiduciary conduct.

All information is provided for educational purposes only. Verify all information against current issuer and index provider publications before drawing any conclusions.

Governing editorial principle

Every word on this site is intended to be descriptive, never prescriptive. The site may state facts: "this fund tracks this index," "the index changed this rule," "this company may qualify for inclusion," "here are the tradeoffs," "here are questions to ask a plan administrator or adviser." It does not state what you should do about any of these facts.

Data sources

Fund-to-index mappings are sourced from issuer prospectuses and fund fact sheets. Concentration data (top-10 weight, mega-cap share) is sourced from issuer daily holdings files — any entry marked "unverified" has not yet been sourced and is flagged as a TODO. Index methodology information is sourced from published methodology documents from Nasdaq Index Services, S&P Dow Jones Indices, CRSP, FTSE Russell, and MSCI. IPO filing information is sourced from SEC EDGAR public records. All source links are provided inline or in the Tracker.

Legal review

The copy on this site has not been reviewed by a securities attorney. A legal review is planned before any paid or personalized feature is added. No payments or personalization are offered in the current version.
