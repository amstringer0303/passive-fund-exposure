About

About this educational index-rule tracker.

What this site is

Passive Fund Exposure is an educational resource for retirement savers who want to understand how index-rule changes affect what their passive funds hold. It was built in response to the Nasdaq-100's adoption of a "fast-entry" rule for large-cap, low-float IPOs in May 2026 — a change that can cause new companies to appear in funds like QQQ and QQQM within weeks of their listing.

What this site is not

This site does not provide investment advice, financial advice, legal advice, or tax advice. It does not recommend any action with respect to any fund, security, or asset.

Governing editorial principle

Every word on this site is intended to be descriptive, never prescriptive. The site may state facts: "this fund tracks this index," "the index changed this rule," "this company may qualify for inclusion," "here are the tradeoffs," "here are questions to ask a plan administrator or adviser." It does not state what you should do about any of these facts.

Data sources

Fund-to-index mappings are sourced from issuer prospectuses and fund fact sheets. Concentration data is sourced from issuer daily holdings files, with unsourced entries flagged as TODOs.

Legal review

The copy on this site has not been reviewed by a securities attorney. A legal review is planned before any paid or personalized feature is added.
